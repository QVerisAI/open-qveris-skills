Preserved concepts include system-change mapping, value-chain-first analysis, scarce-layer tests, evidence grading, candidate challenge, dialogue mode, scorecard factors, and falsification checks.

This adaptation adds standardized QVeris finance CAPs, audited public-document evidence, identity and temporal gates, source independence, causal limits, comparison coverage tiers, evidence-bound scoring, exact Trace/sidecar contracts, and a no-investment-advice boundary.
