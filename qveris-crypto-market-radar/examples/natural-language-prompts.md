# Natural-Language Prompts

- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to make a read-only BTC market snapshot with identity, spot, 30 daily bars, and descriptive technical context.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to compare BTC, ETH, and SOL over the same 30-day window and quote currency, with aligned QVeris evidence and no forecast.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to summarize current crypto market rankings and fear-and-greed context, validating the top five displayed assets.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to check ETH whale activity over the last 24 hours and label accumulation or distribution unsupported unless direction evidence is explicit.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` with `max_calls=3` to return a budget-aware market radar and list the evidence it could not collect.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` in dry-run mode to show the exact QVeris CAP plan without making calls.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to reject a pasted seed phrase without echoing it or making any QVeris call.
- Use `qveris-crypto-market-radar` to compare BTC-USD and BTC-USDT only if quote-basis evidence makes the comparison valid.
